## Intended use
Research and teaching on group differences in resting-state connectivity. **Not a diagnostic or medical device:** a prediction is a model output about one scan, with the accuracy below, and must not inform any decision about a person.

## Limitations
- Modest accuracy: useful for studying group differences, far from individual use.
- One frozen set of 100 gives a wide interval (above); differences of a few points between atlases are within it.
- ABIDE I only: sites, scanners and protocols differ, and no external dataset was tested.
- Trained on all development participants, so a prediction for one of them is not an independent test; demonstrations use frozen-set participants.
