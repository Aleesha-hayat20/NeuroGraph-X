## How to read this

- Differences between sites or quartiles are reported, not tested. With 20 sites and four atlases some differences are expected by chance alone.
- Site, motion and flat regions are not independent: some sites have more motion and more flat regions. A difference along one of them can come from another.
- A site whose accuracy is below its own majority baseline is one where predicting the larger group for everyone would have done as well; this is the clearest sign of where the models are weakest.
- The frozen-set results are not broken down here, on purpose: the frozen set is measured once per model (memo 0011), and subgroup views of it would turn into a second, informal measurement.

## Limitations this supports in the report

- Accuracy is uneven across sites, so a single overall accuracy does not describe every scanner equally well.
- Remaining head motion and flat regions are possible confounds; the models were not adjusted for them (no site harmonisation or motion regression beyond the ABIDE preprocessing).
- Leave-site-out evaluation, which tests a model on a site it never saw, is part of the reliability module planned for FYP-II.
